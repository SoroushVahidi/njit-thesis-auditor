About the NJIT Thesis Auditor

Automated formatting checks to help you prepare for human review.

What it does

Checks a PDF for common formatting issues in margins, spacing, fonts, page numbers, headings, and contents lists. It does not assess your research or grant NJIT approval.

How to use it

  1. Upload a PDF up to {{ max_upload_mb }} MB.
  2. Review each problem, its page, and the suggested fix; open the highlighted PDF when available.
  3. Edit your original document, export a new PDF, and audit it again.
  4. Download the PDF or text report to keep your findings.

What the findings mean

A finding is a prompt to check your document. High, Medium, and Low confidence describe the automated evidence, not approval or certainty.

Understanding the results

Findings appear in page order. Filter by topic, page, or confidence. Counts exclude informational page-number candidates in the Advanced section.

Some findings apply to a whole document or lack a precise highlight. They still appear in reports. “Why is this flagged?” preserves the full correction and background.

Technical rule IDs remain available in optional details, the Rule Reference, and structured exports.

Limitations and human review

This research prototype can miss problems or flag correct formatting. No findings does not mean full compliance. Verify results against NJIT guidelines and complete official human review.

What needs extra review?

Scanned PDFs and poor text extraction limit checks. Some requirements need human judgment; the results page lists coverage and manual checks. A skipped or failed check is not a pass.

For detailed terminology and limitations, see Help.

Privacy and document retention

Your PDF is processed on the configured server. Uploaded files, reports, and highlighted copies are stored temporarily. Anyone possessing a valid result link can access its contents until deletion; share it as carefully as your document.

{{ retention_notice }}

What is stored?

Reports may quote flagged words, headings, or list entries. Highlighted PDFs contain copies of your pages. Downloaded copies remain wherever you save or share them.

Result links do not require a separate sign-in. Temporary retention is not immediate deletion or a guarantee of confidentiality. Only upload documents you are authorized to process on this server.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't my public PDF link work?

Some repositories, including NJIT Digital Commons, may block automated downloads or require a security check. Use your normal authorized access to download the PDF, then upload it here. Login-required and paywalled links are not supported.

Where do I make corrections?

Edit your original Word, LaTeX, or other source document, then export a new PDF. The auditor does not edit your original file.

Which report should I download?

The PDF report combines highlighted pages and findings. The text report is easier to copy. Markdown and JSON are optional exports for other tools.
