How your file is handled. Your uploaded PDF and the reports made from it (including highlighted copies of your pages) are kept in a temporary folder on this server. {{ retention_notice }} Until they are deleted, anyone with your result link can open the results, so share the link only as you would share your document. To point at each problem, your report may quote short pieces of your document, such as table of contents, list, or heading entries and individual flagged words. The server's operational logs record requests and errors, not the contents of your file.
